What Is Nischint

A four-year employability ecosystem, not a one-time workshop.

Nischint by Lightseed is a progressive student development ecosystem that begins in the first year of college and evolves with every academic year. Each stage is designed around the changing developmental, personal and professional needs of students, from self-awareness, communication and emotional resilience, to AI literacy, leadership, workplace readiness and career excellence.

The journey culminates in an intensive Placement Lab, where students don't just prepare for interviews. They emerge as confident, capable professionals ready to contribute from day one.
